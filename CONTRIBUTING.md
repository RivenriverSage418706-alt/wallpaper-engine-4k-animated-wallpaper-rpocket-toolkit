## 🖥️ How to Use the Toolkit

### Adding Your First Wallpaper

1. Click on the "Library" tab on the left side of the main screen.
2. Click the "Add Wallpaper" button in the top right corner.
3. Browse your computer to find an image or video file you want to use as a wallpaper.
4. Select the file and click "Open."
5. Your new wallpaper will appear in your library. Click on it to apply it to your desktop.

### Downloading from Steam Workshop

1. Go to the "Workshop" tab.
2. Use the search bar to find wallpapers by name, tag, or author.
3. Click on any wallpaper you like and then click the "Download" button.
4. Wait for the download to complete. The wallpaper will automatically save to your library.
5. Apply it from your library just like you would any other wallpaper.

### Setting Up Multi-Monitor Layouts

1. Click on the "Layouts" tab.
2. Click "Create New Layout."
3. You'll see a preview of your monitor configuration.
4. Assign a different wallpaper to each monitor by dragging wallpapers from your library onto each monitor preview.
5. Click "Save Layout" and give it a name.
6. You can switch between layouts anytime from the main dashboard.

### Creating PC Profiles

1. Click on the "Profiles" tab.
2. Click "New Profile."
3. Name your profile (e.g., "Gaming," "Work," "Night").
4. Assign wallpapers, layouts, and settings to this profile.
5. Save the profile.
6. To switch profiles, just click on the profile name from the main dashboard and choose which one you want active.

### Managing Android Collections

1. Click on the "Android" tab.
2. Connect your Android device to your PC using a USB cable or make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. The toolkit will detect your device.
4. Create collections by selecting wallpapers from your library and adding them to a new or existing collection.
5. Sync the collection to your phone with one click.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

### Wallpaper Not Showing Up
Make sure the wallpaper file format is supported. This toolkit works best with JPG, PNG, GIF, MP4, and WebM files. If you're using a rare format, try converting it first.

### Application Won't Start
Try running the application as administrator. Right-click on the shortcut and select "Run as administrator." If that doesn't help, restart your computer and try again.

### Multi-Monitor Wallpaper Looks Wrong
Double-check your monitor arrangement in Windows Display Settings. Make sure the toolkit is detecting the correct screen order. You can manually adjust which monitor gets which wallpaper in the Layouts tab.

### Download Speeds Are Slow
This usually depends on your internet connection speed. Try pausing other downloads or streaming to speed things up.
